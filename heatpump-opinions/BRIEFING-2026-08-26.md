# Progress briefing

Current as of 26 August 2026. Companion to `DESIGN.md`, which carries the full
argument and the review questions; this is the short version.

## Action 1: Literature review on network model choice

The review concluded that a **spatial kernel graph** should replace the current
preferential-attachment network, and that most of its parameters can be anchored
on published evidence rather than swept freely.

**Average number of connections.** Central value 15, swept 5–20, from the Dunbar
sympathy group (alters contacted at least monthly). This is an analogue: no study
measures "people whose heating arrangements you hear about."

**Distance decay.** Exponential kernel, lambda = 210 m (half-distance ~145 m),
from Barton-Henry, Wenz & Levermann (2021, *Sci Rep*), which fitted every solar
panel in Fresno from satellite imagery. Swept 100–500 m. US suburban and
roof-visible, so borrowed with caution.

**Connection to similar others.** Yes, but almost entirely through residence
rather than choice. The UK Social Capital Atlas (6 billion friendships) finds
below-median-SES people hold 47% of their friendships with above-median-SES
people, against 50% under random mixing. Homophily *beyond* geography is
therefore set to 0 centrally, swept 0–0.3.

**Long-range ties.** About a third of friendships are independent of geography
(Liben-Nowell et al. 2005, *PNAS*). Central 0.30, swept 0.1–0.5.

**Variation in number of connections.** Degree heterogeneity should arise from
the kernel and local household density, not from preferential attachment. At
m = 3 a Barabasi-Albert graph produces hubs with degree in the hundreds, so a
single hub failure dominates the outcome. BA is retained only as a deliberately
unrealistic comparator.

**Contact rates.** Community Life Survey: 72% of adults in England chat to
neighbours at least monthly, with a gradient of 69% in the most deprived areas
against 76% in the least, and 71% urban against 78% rural. This gradient runs
*against* the hypothesis that word of mouth bites hardest on social estates, and
is stated explicitly rather than left for a referee to find. The long-tie contact
rate has no source and is assumed.

**Validation target.** Cambridge EPRG (CWPE 2566), covering 1.4 million solar PV
systems in England and Wales 2010–2024, finds a positive neighbour effect
pre-2015 (0.128 additional installations per system per local authority after
three months), negative once the adopter pool saturates, and **no measurable
neighbour effect for heat pumps**. This gives the model a falsifiable target and
supports framing the output as a boundary in parameter space rather than a point
estimate.

Two sources — the Fresno fit and the EPRG result — were read from abstracts and
press summaries only; full texts were paywalled.

## Action 2: LSOA data

A national data pipeline is built and run. All sources are Open Government
Licence, covering **all 33,755 English LSOAs**: Census 2021 TS041 (household
counts), TS054 (tenure), TS044 (accommodation type), and the IMD 2025 income
score. No further downloads are required.

Nesta's heat pump suitability layer is dropped as the capability variable: it
correlates -0.91 nationally with the proportion of flats, so the Census
accommodation table carries the same signal on a cleaner licence and, decisively,
varies *within* an LSOA rather than assigning every household an area mean.
Nesta's grid capacity is missing for 1,284 LSOAs (3.8%) in contiguous blocks;
whether to retain Nesta at all is an open question.

From this table a six-type LSOA typology has been derived by k-means on the
model's gate variables (tenure, dwelling type, income). Two types matter most:
social estates — houses (15.2% of LSOAs) and social flats (6.3%). These are used
to construct a stylised plane on which spatial arrangement is swept, with Derby
and Nottingham retained as a calibration reference rather than as the simulation
geography.

## Proposed model changes

None of the following is implemented. It is the design the two actions above
point towards, set out for review.

**Adoption requires five gates at once** — decision right (tenure), capability
(dwelling), opportunity (funding), motivation, and a trigger. Word of mouth acts
on motivation alone, which is also the only fast-moving state: capability is
fixed over a run and opportunity moves slowly. That is why both the dynamics and
the policy lever live there.

**Tenure defines separate adoption channels rather than switching households on
and off.** Owner-occupiers (62.3% of English households) initiate, must pass all
five gates, and are triggered by boiler failure — so a demotivated household
delays rather than being lost. Social tenants (17.1%) do not initiate but can
refuse; the landlord funds the install, handles suitability and sets the timing,
so motivation becomes the dominant remaining gate, alongside disruption, cylinder
space and trust in the landlord. A refusal is a pure loss where the subsidy is
fullest, which makes social housing the sharpest test of the question rather than
inert background. Private renters (20.6%) need the same treatment with a weaker
landlord incentive.

**The influence graph becomes spatial.** The current network is built without
reference to household locations, which makes the correlated-failure axis — the
one the study exists to vary — inert. Construction follows the kernel and
parameters in Action 1 above.

**Geography becomes a stylised plane.** An 8x8 grid of cells, each a real LSOA
sampled from its type and allocated by national frequency, replaces real
coordinates. Arrangement is set by a target spatial autocorrelation and swept,
so segregation becomes an axis rather than a property of one town.

**Trigger events replace the current noise mechanism.** Boiler replacement on a
12–15 year cycle breaks the same adoption stall for a mechanistic rather than a
numerical reason, and sets the timescale of the run.

**Negative word of mouth gets a decay parameter.** A broken-down household is
currently a permanent negative anchor. Whether that damage persists or fades is
one of the things the study needs to vary.

**Quantities to compute before any dynamics are run.** The fraction of
households gated only by motivation, which indicates how much of the population
word of mouth can reach at all; and the expected share of network edges crossing
a cell boundary, which determines whether the arrangement sweep can do anything.
Both fall out of the data and the kernel without simulation, and either could
redirect the compute budget.

**What is data and what is swept.** Data fixes the joint distribution of gates
and how it clusters in space. The word-of-mouth mechanism itself is unmeasured
and belongs on sweep axes: the negative/positive asymmetry, the decay rate,
failure correlation, the quality-response curve, the spatial arrangement, the
initial motivation distribution, and seed adoption. Failure correlation is the
one most worth attention — if bad installs cluster, damage is superlinear in the
failure rate, and quality spend may work chiefly by decorrelating failures rather
than by reducing them.

## Status

Data and typology are implemented and verified. The model changes are proposed
but not built, and nothing has been simulated.
